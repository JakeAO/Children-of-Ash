# "At a Precipice"
## Act 2, Session 7

**Key Characters:** [Vann Solis](/NPCs/Vann%20Solis.md), [Nini Shoju](/NPCs/Nini%20Shoju.md), [ECHO](/NPCs/ECHO.md)  
**Discussed (Off-Screen):** [Proxima Windfall](/NPCs/Proxima%20Windfall.md), [Argosy Brut](/NPCs/Argosy%20Brut.md)

The party returns to [Ashfall](/Factions/PMC/Ashfall.md) HQ with intelligence gathered from Proxima's gala and their encounter with Argosy. They are now confident Proxima is making moves to reignite the [Virelian Civil War](/Events/Virelian%20Civil%20War.md), though his exact motive remains unknown. Seeking more clarity, they directly access the air‑gapped former warmind [ECHO](/NPCs/ECHO.md). ECHO’s information on current events is out of date due to isolation. The party asks if ECHO could inject key information about Proxima's suspected betrayal into the [People's Engine](/Factions/The%20People's%20Engine.md) hivemind; ECHO confirms it could—if reconnected—but warns doing so would expose its continued existence. Pressed on why its destruction mattered, ECHO reveals the suppressed truth: the [Ashfall Incident](/Events/Ashfall%20Incident.md) was not an accidental NHP cascade, but a deliberate People's Engine hivemind command to annihilate the battlefield and force Union intervention to end the war. The party is left with an unresolved moral dilemma about whether to reconnect ECHO and what to do with the truth.

## Details
- **Opening Situation**:
  - Party regroups at Ashfall HQ after prior operations.
  - They consolidate what they learned from the gala and Argosy.
- **Key Conflict / Challenge**:
  - Decide next steps with only circumstantial evidence of Proxima's maneuvering.
  - Weigh acting now versus waiting for stronger proof.
  - Ethical dilemma: reconnect ECHO (risk exposure) or keep it isolated (retain secrecy but lose potential leverage).
- **Notable NPC Interactions**:
  - [Vann Solis] present for strategic discussion.
  - [Nini Shoju] participates in the analysis of gathered information.
  - ECHO explains it can push information to the PE hivemind only if reconnected and that this would reveal its survival.
  - ECHO discloses the Ashfall Incident’s true cause: deliberate PE hivemind orbital bombardment to force outside intervention.
- **Decision Paths Discussed**:
  - Reconnect ECHO and push betrayal data to the PE hivemind.
  - Threaten the PE with revealing the true cause of the Ashfall Incident.
  - Turn ECHO and Proxima-related intelligence over to the Union to let it resolve the situation.
- **Actions Taken**:
  - Party contacts a Union representative over a standard (expected insecure) line claiming knowledge about the Ashfall Incident not publicly known.
  - Intent: provoke responses from Proxima, Cinderknife, and the PE hivemind; accept risk of possible assault on Ashfall HQ.
  - Union agrees to send a delegation to Ashfall HQ.
  - Party reaches out to Argosy to call in a favor, requesting Brazen Thorn support at or near Ashfall HQ in coming days.
- **Twists / Discoveries**:
  - Proxima is actively (reason unknown) working toward reigniting large-scale conflict.
  - Potential to inject information into the PE hivemind exists but carries exposure risk.
  - The accepted narrative of the Ashfall Incident is false; it was intentional, not accidental.
- **Resolution / Aftermath**:
  - No final decision reached on reconnecting ECHO.
  - Awaiting Union delegation arrival and potential Brazen Thorn support.
  - Preparing for possible hostile reaction to the outreach.
